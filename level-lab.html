Privacy Policy – Level Lab

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Level Lab game-building app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, players build and play their own levels. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. Nothing you create, play, or select in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your levels and settings

Levels you create and all app settings are stored only on your device. If you want to share a level, the app gives you a code that you can copy and share however you like, outside the app; anyone with the code can type it into their own copy of the app to play your level. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app — sharing happens only when you choose to pass a code to someone yourself.

In-app purchases

The optional Full Version Unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

Level Lab is designed to be enjoyed by children. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children. Parents: there is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
