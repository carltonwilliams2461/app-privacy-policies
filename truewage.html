TrueWage — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
App: TrueWage (bundle ID com.truewageapp.app)
Publisher: Carlton Williams

The short version

TrueWage collects no data at all. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it makes no network calls — it works fully offline. The wage figures it shows are published U.S. government statistics (Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025) built into the app; nothing about you or your pay ever leaves your device.

What the app stores — and where

Everything TrueWage remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage:

  • The occupation, area, and pay figures you type in to look up a wage distribution or do raise-target math.
  • Any comparisons you choose to save.
  • If you buy the full version, a record that the unlock was purchased (entitlement state), so the app can keep it unlocked.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The optional one-time full-version unlock is processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play. The store handles your payment; TrueWage never sees or stores your payment details. The app only receives the store's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the features.

Children

TrueWage is a general-audience wage information tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
