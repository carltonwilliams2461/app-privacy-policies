TrueCare — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
App: TrueCare (bundle ID com.truecareapp.app)
Publisher: Carlton Williams

The short version

TrueCare collects no data at all. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it makes no network calls — it works fully offline. The cost figures it shows are published by state Medicaid agencies and the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and are built into the app; nothing about you or your family ever leaves your device.

What the app stores — and where

Everything TrueCare remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage:

  • The state, household, and income figures you type in to run the planning tools or the Aid & Attendance worksheet.
  • The family summary sheet you generate — it stays on your device until you choose to copy, download, or print it.
  • If you buy the full version, a record that the unlock was purchased (entitlement state), so the app can keep it unlocked.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The optional one-time full-version unlock is processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play. The store handles your payment; TrueCare never sees or stores your payment details. The app only receives the store's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the features.

Children

TrueCare is a general-audience care-cost information tool for adults planning care. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
