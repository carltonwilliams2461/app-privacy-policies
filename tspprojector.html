Privacy Policy – TSP Projector

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TSP Projector app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, you enter your TSP balance, pay, and contribution rate, and the app projects your balance to retirement using the published agency automatic and matching contribution rules, and shows any match you are not capturing. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. The TSP and IRS rules it calculates with are built into the app itself. Nothing you enter in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your numbers

Your balance, pay, contribution settings, assumptions, and any scenarios you save are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app. Because your numbers live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

The optional TSP Projector Full Version unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Not a government app

TSP Projector is published by Carlton Williams (Williams Apps). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Thrift Savings Plan, the Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board (FRTIB), or any government agency.

Children's privacy

TSP Projector is a retirement-planning calculator for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
