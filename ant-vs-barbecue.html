Privacy Policy – Ant vs Barbecue

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Ant vs Barbecue game ("the app") handles information. The app is sold as a one-time purchase (free download with a one-time full unlock); there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Game progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

In-app purchases

The optional full-game unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play, Apple App Store, or Amazon Appstore). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children

The app is a family-friendly game. Because we collect no information at all, we knowingly collect no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
