NightBook — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
App: NightBook (bundle ID com.nightbookapp.app)
Publisher: Carlton Williams

The short version

NightBook collects no data at all. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it makes no network calls — it works fully offline. The sky it shows is computed on your device from published public-domain data (NASA JPL planetary elements, the Yale Bright Star Catalog, and NASA GSFC eclipse catalogs, all built into the app); nothing about you or where you observe from ever leaves your device.

What the app stores — and where

Everything NightBook remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage:

  • The location you enter (latitude and longitude, and an optional place name you give it). If you tap "Use my location," the device's location is read once, on the device, to fill in those coordinates — it is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Any observing log entries you choose to write.
  • If you buy the full version, a record that the unlock was purchased (entitlement state), so the app can keep it unlocked.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The optional one-time full-version unlock is processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play. The store handles your payment; NightBook never sees or stores your payment details. The app only receives the store's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the features.

Children

NightBook is a general-audience astronomy planner. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
