Privacy Policy – SoundBuddy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SoundBuddy sensory soundboard app ("the app") handles information. The app is sold as a one-time purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All sounds play from files included in the app, and the app works fully offline. Nothing you or your child taps, plays, or selects in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

In-app purchases

The optional full unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play, Apple App Store, or Amazon Appstore). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

SoundBuddy is designed to be enjoyed by children, including in its Calm mode. Because the app collects no information whatsoever, we knowingly collect no personal information from children. Parents: there is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
