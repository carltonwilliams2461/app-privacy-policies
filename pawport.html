Privacy Policy – PawPort

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PawPort pet health record app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, you keep your pets' vaccination records, medications, vet visits, weight, and photos of their paperwork. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. Nothing you enter, record, or photograph in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your pet records and photos

Pet profiles, vaccination records, medication logs, vet visits, weight entries, care notes, pet photos, and photographs of documents (such as rabies certificates and vet invoices) are stored only on your device. If you want a copy of your pet's health summary, the app creates a Kennel Sheet that you can copy or download and share however you like, outside the app. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app — sharing happens only when you choose to send that summary to someone yourself. Because your records live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

The optional PawPort Unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

PawPort is made for pet owners and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
