Privacy Policy – TrueRating

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TrueRating combined-rating calculator app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, you enter your own disability ratings and the app combines them using the published combined ratings table and compensation rate tables, with optional what-if scenarios and a private ratings book. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions. The app is an unofficial helper and is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and no connection to VA, VA.gov, or any government system — the app never asks for your VA credentials or any other credentials. The app works fully offline. The combined ratings table and compensation rate tables it uses are built into the app itself. Nothing you enter in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your ratings records

The conditions, percentages, paired-side marks, and dependent details you enter, and any snapshots you save in your ratings book, are stored only on your device. If you want a copy of your ratings sheet, the app creates it on the phone for you to copy, download, or print and share however you like, outside the app. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app — sharing happens only when you choose to send that sheet to someone yourself. Because your records live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

The optional TrueRating Full Version unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

TrueRating is a calculator and record tool for adult veterans and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
