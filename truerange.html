Privacy Policy – TrueRange

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TrueRange EV Evaluator app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. The app shows electric vehicles on sale in the US with their prices, official EPA range figures, and published highway test results, and it lets you keep a personal log of your own car — drives, charging, and battery health. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. Nothing you enter or record in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your car log and reports

Your drive log, charging log, car details, saved comparisons, and battery health reports are stored only on your device. If you generate a Battery Health Report, the app creates it on your device and it is shared only when you choose to copy, print, or send it yourself, using your device's own tools. The app does not track your location. Because your records live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

The EV catalog

The vehicle catalog (prices, EPA range figures, and published highway test results credited to fueleconomy.gov, Car and Driver, and Consumer Reports) ships inside the app and works fully offline. Browsing it sends nothing anywhere.

In-app purchases

The optional TrueRange Unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

TrueRange is made for car shoppers and EV owners and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
