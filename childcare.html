Childcare Cost Truth — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
App: Childcare Cost Truth (bundle ID com.childcarecostapp.app)
Publisher: Carlton Williams

The short version

Childcare Cost Truth collects no data at all. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it makes no network calls — it works fully offline. The prices it shows are published statistics built into the app (state average childcare prices from Child Care Aware of America's published tables; county medians from the U.S. Department of Labor's National Database of Childcare Prices; tuition figures from The College Board). Nothing about you ever leaves your device.

What the app stores — and where

Almost nothing, and only on your device:

  • If you buy the full version, a record that the unlock was purchased (entitlement state), so the app can keep it unlocked.

The numbers you type into the calculators (an income, a quoted price) are used on the spot, on the device, and are not saved when you leave the screen. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes the entitlement record.

No child records — by design

Childcare Cost Truth is a cost tool only. It contains no child profiles, no names, no birthdays, no tracking, no development logs, and no photos. The only inputs are care arrangements (a setting and an age group), prices, and an optional household income for the affordability math.

Purchases

The optional one-time full-version unlock is processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play. The store handles your payment; Childcare Cost Truth never sees or stores your payment details. The app only receives the store's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the features.

Children

Childcare Cost Truth is a cost-information tool for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
