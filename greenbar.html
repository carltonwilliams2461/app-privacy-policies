Privacy Policy – GreenBar

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the GreenBar — Your Write-Offs expense organizer app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, you keep a hand-entered log of your business spending, organized into potential write-off categories, with benchmark comparisons, a federal tax picture estimate, and a summary you can hand to your tax professional. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and there is no bank login of any kind — entering your spending by hand is the design, on purpose. The app works fully offline. The benchmark and tax tables it uses are built into the app itself. Nothing you enter in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your expense records

Expense entries, mileage entries, notes, recurring templates, business settings, and checklist progress are stored only on your device. If you want a copy of your organizer summary, the app creates it on the phone for you to copy, download, or print and share however you like, outside the app. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app — sharing happens only when you choose to send that summary to someone yourself. Because your records live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchases

The optional GreenBar Full Version unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

GreenBar is a business record tool for adults running a business and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
