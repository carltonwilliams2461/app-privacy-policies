Privacy Policy – TrueTuition

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TrueTuition app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. The app shows the real total cost of college — each college's published sticker price next to the average net price families actually paid, from U.S. Department of Education (IPEDS) data — together with a family playbook covering financial aid, scholarships, athlete and NIL rules, and VA & military education benefits, plus a scholarship and benefit tracker. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. Nothing you enter or select in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your tracker, benefit bank, and cost estimates

The scholarships and education benefits you track, the income-bracket selection you make, the colleges you compare or save, and the benefit amounts you enter in the out-of-pocket calculator are stored only on your device. Your income-bracket selection is never transmitted anywhere — it is used on your device only to highlight the matching price figures. Because your records live only on your device, deleting the app deletes them.

The college dataset

The college dataset (college names, locations, published sticker prices, and average net prices by family-income bracket, sourced from the U.S. Department of Education's IPEDS surveys) ships inside the app and works fully offline. Browsing it sends nothing anywhere. Some playbook pages contain links to outside websites (such as studentaid.gov, va.gov, Fastweb, Bold.org, and Scholarships.com); tapping a link opens that site in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

In-app purchases

The optional TrueTuition unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

TrueTuition is made for families planning and paying for college and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
