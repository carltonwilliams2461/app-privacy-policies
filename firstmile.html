Privacy Policy – FirstMile

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the FirstMile app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. FirstMile is a walk-to-run training coach that builds a personal training plan on your device and adapts it weekly. It offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (FirstMile Premium) processed by the app stores; week 1 of the plan is free. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers run by us.

Information we collect

None transmitted to us. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information to us or to any third party. Everything described below stays on your device.

Location

GPS is used only when you choose GPS mode for an outdoor session, and only while that session is running, to calculate distance and pace. Location data is processed on your device; the session results (distance, pace, and time) are saved only on your device. Location is never sent to us, never shared with third parties, and the app does not track location in the background. The time-only treadmill mode never uses location at all.

Your training data

Your onboarding answers, generated training plans, session logs, and effort ratings are stored only on your device. Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

Training-day reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app uses no push-notification servers.

Subscriptions and payments

The optional FirstMile Premium subscription is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (the Apple App Store or Google Play). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app asks the store on your device whether a subscription is active. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Nothing is sold or shared.

Health note

FirstMile provides general fitness training plans and is not medical advice.

Children's privacy

FirstMile is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we collect no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
