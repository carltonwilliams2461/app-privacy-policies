Privacy Policy – HarvestBook

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the HarvestBook garden journal app ("the app"), developed by Carlton Eugene Williams, handles information. In the app, you keep a private journal of your vegetable garden — gardens, beds and areas, plantings with crop, variety, sow date and seed source, an event log (watered, fed, treated, harvested), harvest weights and counts, and photos. The app offers an optional one-time unlock purchase; there are no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app works fully offline. Nothing you enter, record, or photograph in the app is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Your garden journal and photos

Gardens, beds, plantings, events, harvest records, notes, and photos are stored only on your device. HarvestBook has no social feed, no sharing features, and no chat — nobody else can see your journal. If you want a summary of a season, the app creates a Season Review sheet that you can copy, print, or download and share however you like, outside the app. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server by the app — sharing happens only when you choose to send that sheet to someone yourself. Because your journal lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

The optional HarvestBook Unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

HarvestBook is made for gardeners and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever from anyone, we knowingly collect no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
