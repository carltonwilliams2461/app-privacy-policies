Privacy Policy – FERS Retirement Calculator

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the FERS Retirement Calculator app ("the app") handles information. The app is published as a one-time-purchase product with no subscriptions.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you enter into the calculator — dates, salaries, years of service, and estimates — stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

In-app purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed entirely by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play, Apple App Store, or Amazon Appstore). We never see, handle, or store your payment information. The app store's own privacy policy governs payment processing.

Analytics, advertising, and trackers

The app contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning federal retirement. Because we collect no information at all, we knowingly collect no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
