SubBook — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026
App: SubBook (bundle ID com.subbookapp.app)
Publisher: Carlton Williams

The short version

SubBook collects no data at all. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it makes no network calls with your information — it works fully offline. And the headline: no bank login, ever. SubBook never asks for, sees, or stores bank, card, or streaming-service credentials. You type your subscriptions in by hand; that is the design, on purpose.

What the app stores — and where

Everything SubBook remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage:

  • Your subscription book: the service names, amounts, billing cycles, renewal dates, free-trial dates, categories, and notes you type in.
  • The price history the app keeps when you record that a service raised (or lowered) its price.
  • If you buy the full version, a record that the unlock was purchased (entitlement state), so the app can keep it unlocked.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The cancel guides

The cancel-guide library is built into the app — reading a guide sends nothing anywhere. Each guide's link opens that service's own website in your browser, outside the app, only when you tap it. SubBook performs no cancellations and never contacts a service for you.

Purchases

The optional one-time full-version unlock is processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play. The store handles your payment; SubBook never sees or stores your payment details. The app only receives the store's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the features.

Children

SubBook is a household-finance record tool for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
